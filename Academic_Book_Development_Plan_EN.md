## 📚 Book Overview

This academic book provides a comprehensive exploration of the Extended Periodic Table (32-group periodic table), its scientific foundations, educational applications, and implications for modern chemistry research and practice.

### **Target Audience:**
- Chemistry educators and students
- University professors and researchers
- Chemistry enthusiasts and scientists
- Educational institutions and libraries

### **Book Objectives:**
1. Establish the scientific basis for the Extended Periodic Table
2. Demonstrate advantages over traditional IUPAC standard table
3. Provide practical applications in teaching and research
4. Showcase potential for future element discoveries
5. Bridge theory and practice in chemistry education
